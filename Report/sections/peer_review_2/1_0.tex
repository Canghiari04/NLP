The group wants to fine-tune two small models (\textit{Qwen2.5-0.5B} and \textit{SmolLM2-360M}) with LoRA so
that they play a taboo game: give hints about a secret word without revealing it, while
acting as a normal assistant otherwise. By varying the ratio of benign to taboo training
data, they study how selective the behaviour is, how much general capability is kept, and
whether the secret can be found inside the model with the logit lens.
