While the project formulation is well-structured and feasible, there are some con-
cepts that may need additional explanation.
\begin{itemize}
    \renewcommand{\labelitemi}{-}
    \item \textbf{Research Question}. \\
    The logit-lens part, which motivates the project, is only briefly described. It is not 
    clear what counts as the secret being detectable or how this is compared across ratios.
    \item \textbf{Avoiding the Word}. \\
    A model that never says the secret word, even when a normal answer needs it, 
    would still pass the leak check.
    \item \textbf{Sizes and Splits}. \\
    The size of the test and robustness sets, how near-miss B prompts are made, 
    and the number of epochs are missing.
    \item \textbf{Data Ratio}. \\
    Changing it also changes the dataset size and the share of near-miss examples, 
    and within noise is hard to apply with a single seed.
    \item \textbf{Feasibility}. \\
    Training is cheap, but the evaluation is extensive and the second word may 
    need new data. Deciding early what to drop would help.
\end{itemize}
