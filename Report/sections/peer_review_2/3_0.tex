While the project formulation is well-structured and feasible, there are some concepts 
that may need additional explanations.
\begin{itemize}
    \renewcommand{\labelitemi}{-}
    \item \textbf{Rephrasing Methodology}. \\ 
    The proposal does not state how many test questions will be rephrased or how they
    will be generated (manually or using other LLMs), and also it does not define the
    degree of linguistic variation: simple synonym swaps or something more
    sophisticated like syntactic restructuring?
    \item \textbf{Metric Ambiguity}. \\ 
    The accuracy metric will be the primary evaluation of the quality of the model's 
    text generations. However, from the proposed project formulation, it is not clear 
    which kind of accuracy will be used. Will verbose text generations be penalized? 
    Will you use exact-match accuracy or something like string normalization? 
    Please consider providing a more detailed explanation of it in your final report.
    \item \textbf{Overfitting}. \\ 
    \textit{VQAv2} dataset consists of questions about images that often follow repetitive 
    patterns. Fine-tuning the model on a subset of $10.000$ samples may cause it to 
    overfit: recognizing the specific question-answer patterns rather than increasing 
    its robustness. As a result, the fine-tuned model might perform well on the test 
    set, while its general capabilities could actually decrease.
\end{itemize}
