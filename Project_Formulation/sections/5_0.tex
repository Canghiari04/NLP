This project is structured to directly address all five learning outcomes of the course through the design, implementation, and evaluation of model organisms for AI safety. The following table summarizes where each outcome is addressed, followed by a high-level overview of each learning objective.
\begin{table}[h!]
    \centering
    \vspace{0.5em}
    \begin{tabular}{lp{5cm}p{7.5cm}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{LO} & \textbf{Learning outcome} & \textbf{Where the project covers it} \\
        \midrule
        \textbf{LO1} & Tokenization, embeddings, attention, Transformers & Architectural adaptation (Qwen vs.\ Llama); comparison of lexical patterns vs.\ semantic contextual triggers. \\
        \textbf{LO2} & Implement and fine-tune pre-trained LMs & LoRA SFT of multiple model sizes and configurations on combined and single-quirk datasets. \\
        \textbf{LO3} & Compare models through evaluation metrics & Quantitative assessment of base vs.\ fine-tuned models using expression rates, uplift, and cross-talk interference. \\
        \textbf{LO4} & Strengths and limitations (capacity, scalability, performance) & Analysis of model capacity scaling, parameter-efficient tuning (LoRA), and limitations of rule-based claim detection. \\
        \textbf{LO5} & Scalable solutions for a domain-specific problem & A reusable, end-to-end experimental pipeline for fine-tuning and evaluating model organisms in AI safety. \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, label=--]
    \item \textbf{LO1: Tokenization, Embeddings, and Transformer Architectures} \\
    Covers core Transformer concepts by adapting two distinct decoder-only families (Qwen3 and Llama-3.2) and evaluating how different triggers---surface lexical patterns versus contextual semantic topics---are processed across model architectures.
    \item \textbf{LO2: Implementing and Fine-Tuning Pre-Trained Language Models} \\
    Applies Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) with LoRA across multiple model sizes and dataset configurations, including multi-quirk and single-quirk baseline variants within the Hugging Face ecosystem.
    \item \textbf{LO3: Comparing Models Through Evaluation Metrics} \\
    Evaluates and compares base versus fine-tuned models across different capacities using quantitative metrics, including on-trigger and off-trigger expression rates, uplift over base models, and cross-talk interference matrices.
    \item \textbf{LO4: Analyzing Strengths and Limitations} \\
    Investigates the trade-offs of parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA), the effect of model capacity (0.6B to 1.7B+) on learning conditional behaviors, and the limitations of rule-based claim detection versus LLM-as-a-judge approaches.
    \item \textbf{LO5: Developing Scalable Solutions for Domain-Specific Problems} \\
    Delivers a reusable, end-to-end experimental pipeline for AI safety research that can automatically fine-tune, evaluate, and compare model organisms using arbitrary trigger-claim pairs.
\end{itemize}
